# Radical Pairs — Classification [AVÉRÉ] vs [HYPOTHÈSE]

**Date :** 2025-11-13  
**Purpose :** Transparent classification of radical pair quantum effects: demonstrated vs proposed  
**Rationale :** Radical pair mechanisms are **controversial** in quantum biology. This document distinguishes **direct evidence** from **inference**.

---

## Classification Criteria

### [AVÉRÉ] — Direct Demonstrations
✅ **Peer-reviewed publication** (Nature, Science, PNAS, or specialist journals)  
✅ **Quantitative observable** measured (MFE%, singlet/triplet yield, timescale)  
✅ **Reproducible** by independent labs  
✅ **In vitro or in vivo demonstration** (not just theoretical model)

### [HYPOTHÈSE] — Indirect Inferences
⚠️ **Behavioral data only** (no molecular-level measurement)  
⚠️ **Theoretical model** without direct experimental test  
⚠️ **Single-lab result** (not reproduced)  
⚠️ **Correlation ≠ causation** (e.g., knockout phenotype ambiguous)

## Systems Classification

### 1. Cryptochrome (Avian Magnetoreception)

| System | Observable | Evidence Type | Classification | DOI |
|--------|------------|---------------|----------------|-----|
| **Robin Cry4 (in vitro)** | MFE 50 µT sensitivity | Direct MFE measurement | ✅ **[AVÉRÉ]** | [10.1038/s41586-021-03618-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03618-9) |
| **Robin Cry4 (in vivo magnetoreception)** | Behavioral orientation | Behavioral + retina localization | ⚠️ **[HYPOTHÈSE FORTE]** | Same paper + behavioral studies |
| **Drosophila Cry1a** | Anisotropy change | Indirect inference (review) | ⚠️ **[HYPOTHÈSE]** | [10.1146/annurev-biophys-032116-094545](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-biophys-032116-094545) |

**Consensus :**
- ✅ **[AVÉRÉ]** : Cryptochrome radical pairs show MFE in vitro (robin Cry4, Xu et al. 2021)
- ⚠️ **[HYPOTHÈSE FORTE]** : Cryptochrome = magnetoreceptor in vivo (high confidence but causality not proven)
- ⚠️ **[HYPOTHÈSE]** : Drosophila Cry1a MFE (inferred from behavioral assays, no direct MFE% measured)

**Gap :** Need in vivo MFE measurement in behaving bird (currently impossible, non-invasive method needed)

| **E. coli CPD photolyase** | FAD-TrpH radical pair | Direct EPR, transient absorption | ✅ **[AVÉRÉ]** | [10.1021/ja203749t](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja203749t) |

**Consensus :**
- ✅ **[AVÉRÉ]** : FAD-Trp radical pair mechanism in photolyase (EPR confirmed, multiple labs)

- ⚠️ **[HYPOTHÈSE]** : Quantum coherence enhances repair (high efficiency ≠ proof, classical hopping also explains data)

**Gap :** Need direct coherence measurement (T2) in operating photolyase, not just kinetics

### 3. Photosystem II (PSII)

| System | Observable | Evidence Type | Classification | DOI |
|--------|------------|---------------|----------------|-----|
| **PSII D1-D2 radical pair** | P680•+ PheoD1•- charge separation | Direct spectroscopy | ✅ **[AVÉRÉ]** | [10.1073/pnas.1316207110](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1316207110) |
| **Singlet yield modulation** | ~100ns timescale | Transient absorption kinetics | ✅ **[AVÉRÉ]** | Same paper |
| **Quantum coherence in photosynthesis** | 2D electronic spectroscopy | Controversial (artifacts debated) | ⚠️ **[HYPOTHÈSE DÉBATTUE]** | [10.1038/nature05678](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature05678) (FMO, not PSII) |

**Consensus :**
- ✅ **[AVÉRÉ]** : PSII radical pair P680•+ PheoD1•- (charge separation confirmed, foundational biophysics)
- ✅ **[AVÉRÉ]** : Singlet yield ~100ns timescale (transient absorption standard method)
- ⚠️ **[HYPOTHÈSE DÉBATTUE]** : Quantum coherence enhances photosynthesis efficiency (18+ years debate, no consensus)

**Gap :** FMO complex (separate from PSII) coherence still debated. PSII coherence less studied, needs replication.

- ✅ **[AVÉRÉ]** : Bacterial RC radical pair mechanism (foundational, Nobel-adjacent work, Deisenhofer et al. 1988)

**No controversy** — bacterial RC = gold standard radical pair system, fully characterized

## Recommendations for Communication

### For Scientific Publications
**Use this language :**
- ✅ "Radical pair mechanism demonstrated in vitro (cryptochrome, photolyase, bacterial RC)"
- ⚠️ "Quantum coherence in biological function remains hypothetical (photolyase, PSII)"
- ⚠️ "Cryptochrome magnetoreception strongly supported but not causally proven"

**Avoid :**
- ❌ "Quantum biology is proven" (overgeneralization)
- ❌ "Birds use quantum mechanics to navigate" (causal link not established)
- ❌ "Photosynthesis requires quantum coherence" (classical explanations sufficient)

### For Public Communication
**Accurate framing :**
- "Some proteins form radical pairs that are sensitive to magnetic fields (demonstrated)"
- "Whether this explains bird navigation is still being investigated (hypothesis)"
- "Quantum effects may play a role, but proof requires more experiments"
